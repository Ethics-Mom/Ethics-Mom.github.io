# Psychoanalysis Adendum 1

## A few things...
after ruminating further on the system I have made a couple decisions.  
• The anticipatory nature of Phobos is much better suited to a mental capability, rather than a drive.  
• I also decided to add Thanatos back because it left a hole in its absense.  
• I have debated spliting eros on pedestrian desire vs libido but i decided agaisnt it.  
• I am still mulling over splitting Psyche, it's quite large but produces similar feelings.

## The current System

I have kept Possession and Overdetermination, here's the drives:

### Eros (The Bodily Drive)

At the core of Eros is the body and it's demands.  
Eros is the bodily desires, hunger, sexual feelings, and entertainment. It is also the desire to do nothing and to stay asleep.  
In possession it manifests as a complete hedonistic haze.  
In positive expression it leads to ecstatic, restful, or satiated feelings.  
In negative expression it leads to anger and moodiness.

### Mania (The Hubris Drive)

The core of Mania is the Ego, its defense, and its exertion.  
Mania is the aggressive drive, the desire for power, rage, power-tripping / mania, and power fantasy. It is also the driver of tribalist feelings of in-group and out-group.
Additionally Mania can drive defensiveness in conversation, as well as principals "I am too good for this".  
In possession it manifests as a power tripping or manic episode.  
In positive expression it leads to feelings of power, self esteem, and confidence.  
In negative expression it leads to feelings of humbled shame or spite.

### Thanatos (The Death Drive)

The core of Thanatos is survival, and ensuring it at all costs.  
Thanatos is the imp of the perverse, the call of the void, the void that tells you to act.
It pushes the mind to consider future possibilites as well as emotionally distancing the mind during action.  
In possession it manifests as a violent or absent "hypnosis" (perhaps as the momentum of fear).  
In positive expression it stays quiet, you feel safe.  
In negative expression it leads to gripping fear and the ceasing of logical thought.

### Techne (The Diligence Drive)

At the core of Techne is the nervous energy to do things as human survival instinct to keep moving.  
Techne is the fulfillment drive, the drive to work, to solve problems, to order, to know and learn. It's the part satisfied by craftsmanship and skill, as well as the antsy side.  
In possession it manifests as the flow state.  
In positive expression it brings feelings of fulfillment and satisfaction.  
In negative expression it leads to antsyness and anxiety.

### Psyche (The Soul Drive)

At the core of Psyche is the innermost mind, the point of view, and the sense of self.  
Psyche is on one hand the introspective side that drives feelings around Identity and actualization, and on another hand the social part that empathizes, finds kinship, and romance.  
In possession it manifests as an enlightened state.  
In positive expression it brings feelings of melancholy and disphoria.  
In negative expression it leads to feelings of oneness, interconnectedness, and euphoria.
